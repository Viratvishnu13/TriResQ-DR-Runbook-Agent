## 1 Protection schedule
Finance Production is protected by a daily full backup, incremental backups and storage replication.

## 2 Recovery point unavailable
If no usable recovery point can be confirmed, stop recovery, record RPO as not measured, and escalate to the Backup and Storage owner. Do not estimate the recovery point.
